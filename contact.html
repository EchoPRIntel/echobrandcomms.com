Good to know

Before we talk.

What does Echo do?
Echo is a strategic communications and PR firm that helps bold brands and leaders get seen, heard and remembered. We build campaigns that earn trust, drive growth and make your message matter.
Who is Echo for?
Founders, CMOs and companies ready to lead rather than follow. If you have something real to say, Echo helps you say it louder, smarter and more powerfully.
How is Echo different?
We don’t chase mentions, we build momentum. Every strategy is sharp, tailored and personal. No fluff: stories that land, leaders who resonate and results that move markets.
Can you help me win awards or speak at industry events?
Yes. We build award strategies and secure high-impact speaking slots that create third-party credibility and get your name into the right rooms.
Do you work globally?
Yes. We have led campaigns across 20+ markets, securing top-tier coverage and recognition for clients in the US, EU, UK, MEA, APAC and LATAM.
Do you work with early-stage companies?
Yes. If you have a strong product and a bold story, we will help you tell it like a category leader from day one.
